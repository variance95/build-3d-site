Finding us.

Tucked into the North Pennines, a few minutes off the main dale road.

A rough layout of the site — where the pitches, facilities and dog field sit, and the walk between them. Full directions and the postcode for satnav are sent with your booking confirmation.

By roadOff the main dale road (A689), signed toward the moor. Follow it two miles past the second cattle grid.
Nearest townStanhope, 4 miles — shops, fuel and a pub if you need to stock up first.
SatnavPostcodes in this part of the dale can lead you astray by a field or two. We'll send exact directions with your booking.
By public transportNorth Pennines Motor Services runs to Stanhope; we can collect from there by arrangement.
