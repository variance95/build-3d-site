A website only works if it says what you do and loads fast — a lot of them don't, because they're built on a stack of plugins and a theme nobody really chose. We build from scratch in plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, so there's nothing extra weighing the page down and nothing to untangle later when something needs to change.

Whether that's a simple one-pager that explains the business and gets people in touch, or something with more moving parts — bookings, a menu, a shop — the site does the job you actually need, not the job a template assumes you need.

Business & product sites

A site that explains what you do clearly enough that someone understands it in a few seconds, and makes it obvious what to do next. Usually a home page, an about or story page, what you offer, and a contact method that actually gets used — built around your own structure, not a template's.

Landing pages

One page, one job — a launch, a single product, an event, a limited-time offer. No navigation to get lost in, just the case for the one thing you want someone to do: book, buy, sign up, or get in touch. Quicker to build and quicker to launch than a full site, and easy to retire once the campaign's done.

Refreshes & rebuilds

Replacing a dated, slow, or hard-to-update site without starting from zero. We keep what the old one got right — the copy, the structure, what people actually land on it looking for — and rebuild the parts that don't: usually the speed, the mobile experience, or how easy it is to change something yourself afterwards.
