Your Stay

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Simple facilities, kept clean and close to the pitches — here's what's where.

By the farmhouse

Showers & toilets

Hot water, cleaned twice daily in season. Two shower rooms, key-code entry, lit after dark.

Next door

Laundry & washing-up

Coin-op washer and a covered dish-washing sink, undercover if it's raining.

In the old dairy

Small farm shop

Eggs, milk, firewood and a few basics. Self-serve, honesty box after 6pm.

At the barn

Wifi at the barn

One reliable spot by the barn for calls or a quick email — the pitches themselves are properly off-grid.

By the barn

Fire pit

A shared pit near the barn, wood provided, lit most evenings when it's dry enough.

Past the gate

Dog walking field

A fenced paddock away from the working land, gate latches properly, poo bags provided. It's moved around depending on what the fields are being used for that season — ask at the farmhouse for this week's spot.
