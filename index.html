RescueDB

Our management system for animal rescues — a complete system that runs on the rescue's own hardware, built and refined in a real, working rescue. Plans start at £240/month on a 12-month minimum term, rolling monthly after that; if the plan later ends, RescueDB carries on as a licence-free version — fully working, every record intact, just without updates and support.

3D printing

Made-to-order 3D printing — practical kit designed for rescues, custom one-offs, and creative pieces. A few things we've designed:

Halo reader holder

A purpose-made holder for the Halo microchip reader — keeps it secure, charged and to hand where staff need it, instead of going missing in a drawer. Printable plain or branded to your organisation, both shown.

The Universal Name Plate

A modular, cage-mounted holder for a printed 6×4″ card — the animal's name, photo, medical flags and care notes, at a glance on the front of every pen. And it's built into RescueDB: export any animal and out comes a print-ready 6×4 that slots straight in. They screw on, rearrangeable, and printed to fit your cages.

Lithophane lantern
