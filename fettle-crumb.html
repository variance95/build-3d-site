Our story

Started with one oven and a stubborn sourdough starter.

Fettle & Crumb opened in 2022 in a tiny unit just off the market square — one oven, three tables, and a starter that took a very stubborn six weeks to get going. It's grown a bit since, but the way we bake hasn't changed: slow, by hand, no shortcuts.
