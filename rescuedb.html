01 Why RescueDB

Built in a rescue, not in the abstract.

Most rescues run on a patchwork of spreadsheets, paper, and memory — or pay every month for generic shelter software built for somewhere else.

RescueDB is a complete animal-management system shaped around how your rescue actually works — everything from daily care rounds through to adoption outcomes, in one place. Built and refined in a live, working rescue.

02 What it does

Everything the rescue runs on, in one place.

From the morning meds round to the year-end report, it's all one system — here's what that looks like.

Daily care rounds

The morning round, on a screen. Every medication checked off per shift and per animal — given, skipped with a reason, or still pending — colour-coded so the floor sees at a glance what's done and what's left. Review any past date, or print the round sheet.

A record for every animal

One permanent profile per animal — photos, history, microchip and vaccination details, bonded pairs, allergies, and the full adoption and foster history. It stays on file even after they've been rehomed.

Medications & medical history

Per-animal medication schedules, weight trends graphed over time, and a complete medical log — vet visits, treatments, and records imported straight from a PDF. A built-in drug reference covers every medication and diet in use, and upcoming appointments are flagged on the animal's profile.

Statistics & reporting

A year-by-year dashboard — intakes, adoptions, average days in rescue, and species & breed breakdowns — with role-based access, a full audit trail, nightly backups and PDF export.

Cage-ready cards, built in.

Export any animal as a print-ready 6×4″ card that drops straight into our 3D-printed Universal Name Plate — every animal's details, right on the front of the pen.

The mobile app

Care rounds, in your pocket.

Staff run the daily round straight from their phone — checking off medications at the kennel, logging weights and health notes by the pen, snapping photos on the spot. It keeps working with no signal and syncs back to your server the moment it's in range.

04 How working together looks

Straightforward to start — no in-house tech needed.

1

Setup

A small dedicated server is installed on-site and configured on your network, staff are trained on the system, and your existing records are checked so they can be imported cleanly.

2

Ongoing support

Maintenance and monitoring, regular updates and improvements, prompt response to issues, and new features developed in line with how your rescue evolves.

Importing your existing records can be handled by your own team to keep costs down, or quoted separately if you'd like it done for you. Either way, your data is checked and a clean import is confirmed before you're left to it — so nothing gets lost in the move.
