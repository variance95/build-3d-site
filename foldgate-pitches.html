Your Stay

Two ways to stay.

Come for a few nights of stargazing, or park up properly if you're on the road long-term. There's a pitch for both.

01

Open-sky pitches

Set away from any farm lighting and facing open south sky. Hookup's there if you want it, but most stargazers don't bother.

  • Unobstructed horizon, no light spill
  • Seasonal sighting guide included
  • 2–5 night stays
02

Long-stay motorhome

Proper hookup, hardstanding, and enough elbow room to actually work from the van for a week or a month.

  • 16A hookup, fresh water, waste point
  • One quiet spot with reliable signal, for calls
  • Weekly and monthly rates
