Web design

Do you use templates or page builders?

No — every site is built from scratch in plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript. No theme, no plugin stack, nothing extra weighing the page down, and nothing to untangle later when something needs to change.

See how we build →

What's included in a business or product site?

Usually a home page, an about or story page, what you offer, and a contact method that actually gets used — built around your own structure, not a template's.

I only need one page for a launch — is that too small a job?

Not at all — that's a landing page. One focused page doing one job: getting people to book, buy, sign up or get in touch. Quicker to build and launch than a full site, and easy to retire once it's done its job.

Can you replace my existing site without losing what works?

Yes — that's a refresh or rebuild. We keep what the old site got right (the copy, the structure, what people actually land on it looking for) and rebuild the parts that don't: usually the speed, the mobile experience, or how easy it is to change something yourself afterwards.

3D printing

What kind of things can you print?

Custom and one-off objects designed around a specific need, replacement or spare parts, and prototypes or small runs. If it can be printed, we'll help you make it.

See what we make →

Can you recreate a broken or discontinued part?

Usually, yes. Send us the broken part, a photo, or rough measurements — we model it, print a test fit to check, then print the finished piece. Most jobs like this are just the one part.

Do you only do one-offs, or small batches too?

Both. We can iterate a design over a few test prints, then print a proper batch once it's right — cage tags for a whole shelter, a fixture for every van in a fleet, ten of something instead of one.
