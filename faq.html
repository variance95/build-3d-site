RescueDB — the basics

What is RescueDB?

At its heart, RescueDB is a database management system built for animal rescues — a single, reliable place for every animal's record. But it's much more than a list: it also handles medications, daily care rounds, weights, health notes, photos and adoptions, turning those records into the tools you use to run the rescue day to day.

Anyone on the team, from a new volunteer to a manager, can pick it up and use it on a phone, tablet or computer.

See what it does →

What hardware do we need?

Just a small computer (a Raspberry Pi) that sits in your office and serves the app over your own wifi. We set it up for you. Staff and volunteers then use it from whatever devices you already have — no special equipment, no installs on each device.

Can volunteers and all staff use it, or just managers?

Everyone. There are roles — staff, manager and admin — so people see the right things and sensitive settings stay protected, but the everyday jobs (care rounds, meds, health notes) are open to the whole team. Volunteers can use a quick 4-digit PIN to log in.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. It runs on your own network, so it doesn't depend on an internet connection at all. And if a device drops off the wifi out in the kennels, it keeps working and syncs back up once it's reconnected — nothing gets lost.

Your data & security

Where does our data live — and who can see it?

On your own hardware, in your building. Your records never leave the premises and aren't stored in anyone's cloud — not even ours. There's no third party holding your data and no one else with access to it.

Are our records backed up?

Yes — the system makes automatic daily backups, and you can take a manual backup or a full data export whenever you like. Optional off-site backup can be set up too, so a copy lives safely away from the building.

Can we get our data out if we ever want to leave?

Always. It's your data, and you can export the lot at any time. We're not in the business of holding anyone hostage.

Cost & support

What does it cost?

There's a one-off setup fee and a monthly plan — no per-user charges and no cloud fees on top. We keep it deliberately simple and affordable for rescues. The full breakdown is on the RescueDB page.

What happens if we stop paying?

Your system keeps working. If a plan ends, RescueDB carries on running as a licence-free version with all your data intact — you just stop receiving updates, new features and support. Nothing is taken away, and your records are always yours to export.

What support do we get?

A real person — me — who knows the system inside out. No call centre, no ticket queue. While you're on a plan you get help when you need it, plus updates and new features as they're built. The app can even update itself with a single tap when there's a new version.

Is there a minimum term?

Plans run on a 12-month minimum term. That covers the setup and onboarding work properly and lets us support you well through your first year. After that it carries on month to month.

You do 3D printing as well?

We do — practical items, replacement parts and creative one-offs, all made to order. For rescues and vets we make a purpose-built holder for the Halo microchip reader, and our Universal Name Plate prints straight from a RescueDB animal record.
